About the Astrologer

P.C. Mohapatra

Scholar & Jyotishi · M.A., B.Ed., PGDHE, DDE

P.C. Mohapatra is a respected Vedic astrologer based in Radhamadhab Nagar, Bhubaneswar, Odisha. A National Award-Winning Teacher and Gold Medallist in Astrology, he has dedicated over five decades to the authentic science of Jyotish Shastra.

As the Founder of Swami Sivananda Astro Research Centre (Est. 1975), he has helped thousands of families navigate life's most important decisions with clarity, compassion, and unwavering confidentiality.
