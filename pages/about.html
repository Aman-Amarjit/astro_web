Founder & Chief Astrologer

P.C. Mohapatra

Scholar & Jyotishi · M.A., B.Ed., PGDHE, DDE

P.C. Mohapatra is a respected Vedic astrologer based in Radhamadhab Nagar, Bhubaneswar, Odisha. A National Award-Winning Teacher and Gold Medallist in Astrology, he founded the Swami Sivananda Astro Research Centre in 1975 and has been forecasting since 1970.

With qualifications M.A., B.Ed., PGDHE, DDE and over five decades of dedicated practice in Jyotish Shastra, he offers authentic Janam Kundli readings and expert guidance on career, health, marriage, relationships, and finance.

Every consultation is conducted with the utmost care, confidentiality, and deep respect for the ancient traditions of Vedic astrology.
